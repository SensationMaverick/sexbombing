FAQ

Good questions

When and where is it?

June 2027, in Lincolnshire, close to the M18 corridor. The exact date, checkpoint and venue are shared with ticket holders only.

How do I get tickets?

Tickets are sold through our partner SpicyMatch. Couples tickets and a limited number of singles tickets will be available. Prices will be announced before they go on sale.

Who can come?

Anyone aged 18 or over. It's couples-led, with around 10% of places for singles of any gender, including trans and non-binary guests.

Do I have to join in the road trip?

You do have to arrive at the checkpoint for 6pm, because the convoy is how we all arrive. The challenges and points are optional, but they're half the fun.

Do I have to do anything in the play rooms?

No. They're optional, staffed, and consent-led. You can enjoy the DJ, the bar and the company and never go near them.

What is the consent policy?

Ask first, accept no as a complete answer, and respect it immediately. Staff and marshals are on hand all night, and anyone who breaks the rules is removed without refund.

Can I take photos?

Not inside the play rooms or anywhere that's marked no-phones. Never post or share photos of other guests without their clear permission.

Where do I sleep?

Camp on site, bring a camper, or stay in a local hotel. We have a taxi partnership so nobody has to drink and drive.

What if I can't make it?

Refund and transfer terms will be published when tickets go on sale.

Why the plain packaging?

Your pack is posted in plain, unmarked packaging so nothing on the outside reveals what's inside.
