The journey

It starts the moment you book

Most parties begin at the door. This one begins on your driveway.

  1. Book

    Grab a couple's or singles ticket through SpicyMatch and your points start counting straight away.

  2. Get your pack

    A plain-wrapped pack lands on your doormat: flag, stickers, wristband and your road trip card.

  3. Hit the road

    Drive from wherever you live, solo or with a group of friends, completing challenges for points on the way.

  4. Checkpoint, 6pm

    Everyone meets at a secret checkpoint. Arrive however long it took you. Tell your story, claim your points.

  5. The convoy

    A marshalled convoy of 100-odd cars rolls out to the main venue together.

  6. The party

    The DJ is already playing. Park up, grab a drink, and the night is yours.

The night

Big sound. Your pace.

A proper headline-style DJ set on a proper sound system, with space to dance, chat, or slip away. No pressure, ever.

DJ-led dance floor

A mainstream name behind the decks, playing from the moment the convoy arrives.

Play rooms

Private, staffed spaces on site for those who want them. Entirely optional, always consent-led.

Camp or crash

Pitch a tent or park up a camper on site. Prefer a bed? Local hotels and a taxi partnership keep everyone safe getting there.

One night only

No week-long commitment. Arrive, party, sleep, go home with stories and a sticker on the car.

Who it's for

Couples first. Singles welcome.

About nine in ten places go to couples. A limited number of singles tickets are open to women, men, trans and non-binary guests, so the room stays balanced and friendly.

In your pack

Everything for the drive

  • Car flag
  • Sticker sheet
  • Air freshener
  • Event wristband
  • Road trip points card
  • Convoy rules and route card

T-shirts, hoodies, caps and bags will be available to buy at the checkpoint and the venue. Packs arrive in plain, unmarked packaging.
