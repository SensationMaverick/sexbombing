The road trip

Earn points from your front door

Your points start the minute you book. Every challenge is optional, every challenge is photo-friendly, and none of them involve driving badly.

How points work

  • Before you set off. Stick the stickers on the car, share your pack on socials, and tag a friend who should be coming.
  • On the road. Work through your road trip card: a landmark, a services stop, a roadside picnic, a wave to another Sex Bombing car.
  • Flash for points. Spot another car with the flag? Flash your headlights, and each log a sighting.
  • At the checkpoint. Best-dressed car, best flag, best group, furthest travelled.
  • Share it. Post with the event hashtag and tag the official account. We pull posts together to score the leaderboard.

Top of the leaderboard wins prizes on the night. Group captains who bring five or more guests get bonus points.

Convoy rules

Fun, but safe

  • Drivers never use a phone or take photos while driving. Passengers shoot, or you wait for a stop.
  • No racing, no stunts, no blocking junctions. Normal road rules apply at all times.
  • Follow the marshals at the checkpoint and stay with your assigned convoy group.
  • Drink at the venue, not before. Plan your way home: taxi, hotel, or camp on site.
  • Leave nothing behind at stops, and be considerate to everyone you pass.
